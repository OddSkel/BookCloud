# Project – Phase 1  
## Dataset Identification, Business Capabilities and Use Cases  

---

# 1. Dataset Identification

**Name:** Goodreads Cleaned Dataset  

**URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ishanrealstate/goodreads-cleaned-dataset

**Topic:** Books metadata, genres, ratings and popularity analytics  

**Original Formats Available:** Parquet, CSV and TXT  

**Processing Format for the Project:** All Parquet files will be converted to CSV (UTF-8 plain text) before ingestion into the database.

**Size:** 1.78M+ records 

**Date of Release:** 30/01/2026 (Kaggle publication)

The dataset contains structured information about books, genres, ratings, popularity and publication year.

---

# 2. Business Capabilities

The project simulates a startup offering **Book Market Intelligence and Recommendation Services** through cloud-native services.

The main business capabilities are:

1. Literary Market Intelligence  
2. Smart Book Discovery & Recommendation  
3. Author Performance Analytics  
4. Popularity vs Quality & Publishing Evolution Analytics  

---

# 3. Services and Use Cases


## Service 1 — Genre Intelligence & Market Trends

### Objective
Analyze how book genres evolve over time and identify the most popular and highest-rated genres.

### Use Cases
- Rank genres by: 
    - Average rating
    - Total number of ratings
- Analyze genre growth or decline over time
- Study genre popularity trends over time

---

## Service 2 — Smart Book Discovery & Recommendation

### Objective
Provide intelligent book search and recommendation features for users.

### Use Cases
- Search books by title, author or keywords from summaries
- Recommend similar books based on:
  - Genre overlap
  - Rating similarity
  - Popularity profile
---

## Service 3 — Author Performance Analytics

### Objective
Evaluate and compare authors based on ratings, popularity and performance trends.

### Use Cases
- Rank authors by:
    - Average rating
    - Total ratings
- Analyze author performance evolution over time
- Identify most consistent authors
- Detect authors with highest growth
---

## Service 4 — Popularity vs Quality

### Objective
Compare book popularity with perceived quality, and analysing long-term publishing trends.

### Use Cases
- Identify: 
    - High popularity, low rating books
    - High rating, low popularity books
- Analyze correlation between ratings and number of reviews
- Study publishing growth over time
- Compare classical vs modern literature
